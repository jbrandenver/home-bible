## Accessibility at <Product / Organization>

We want everyone to be able to use <Product>. We design and build with
accessibility as a core requirement and work to conform to recognized standards.

**Standard we target.** We aim to meet **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines
(WCAG) 2.2 Level AA**. <If a law applies, add: "As a <state/local government /
federal / EU> service, we also work toward <WCAG 2.1 AA under the ADA Title II
rule / WCAG 2.0 AA under Section 508 / EN 301 549 under the European Accessibility
Act>."> Conformance is an ongoing effort, not a one-time certification.

**How we test.** We test with a combination of automated checks and manual
testing, including keyboard-only use and screen readers (<e.g., VoiceOver on
macOS/iOS, NVDA on Windows, TalkBack on Android>). Automated tools alone do not
prove accessibility, so manual testing is part of our process.
