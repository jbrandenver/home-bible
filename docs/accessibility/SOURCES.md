# Accessibility — Authoritative Sources

Primary sources consulted for this standard. Prefer these (government, W3C,
platform vendors) over accessibility-vendor or law-firm marketing pages.
**Laws and standards change — reverify before relying on a date or deadline.**

Legend for "Type": **Law** (statute) · **Regulation** (binding rule) ·
**Standard** (normative technical standard) · **Guidance** (non-binding) ·
**Best practice**.

_All URLs accessed: 2026-07-22._

## How to reverify (do this before quoting a deadline or standard)

1. Confirm WCAG version/status at https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ (and check for a
   later WCAG or WCAG 3.0 draft status).
2. Confirm ADA Title II dates at ada.gov (rule + any interim final rule).
3. Confirm Section 508 standard at access-board.gov / section508.gov.
4. Confirm Colorado technical standard + deadlines directly with CO OIT.
5. Confirm EAA/EN 301 549 status and the current EN version for the member
   state(s) in scope.
6. For any legal decision, obtain qualified legal counsel — this file is
   engineering research, not legal advice.
